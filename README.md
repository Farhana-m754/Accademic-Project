Project Title: Women as Financial Architects - Role of Female-Headed Households in Optimizing Foreign Remittance Utilization. About the Project:
This is an academic research project focused on understanding how female-headed households manage and utilize foreign remittances. The study explores women's role as financial decision-makers in improving household economic welfare.Objective:
To analyze the expenditure pattern, savings, and investment behavior of households receiving foreign remittances, which are led by women.Methodology:Conducted primary data collection through a structured survey of 60 respondents in Kerala.Data cleaning, organization, and analysis were performed using Microsoft Excel.Used statistical tools like Median and Standard Deviation to understand spending variations.Analyzed categories: Household consumption, Children's education, Healthcare, Savings, Debt repayment, and Productive investments.Key Insights:Identified how remittances are allocated across essential needs vs. future investments.Found significant contribution of women in ensuring financial stability and optimizing remittance use for long-term welfare.Visualized data trends using charts and graphs to highlight financial patterns.Tools Used: Microsoft Excel | Data Cleaning | Descriptive Statistics | Data Visualization | Primary Research
